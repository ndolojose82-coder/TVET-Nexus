About TVET Nexus

TVET Nexus Hub is a free study resource for TVET students taking ICT courses in Kenya. It brings Module 1 and Module 2 revision material together in one place so you can study from your phone or laptop.

What you will find here

Study notes

Downloadable PDF notes for the Module 1 and Module 2 units.

Revision questions

Practice questions with short answers for every unit.

Quiz generator

Pick a unit and get a random set of questions to test yourself.

Who it is for

Students preparing for ICT exams at Module 1 and Module 2 level, and anyone who wants a quick way to revise key topics.

Our content

The revision questions on this site are original and written for revision. They are not KNEC past papers, so always check answers against your class notes and your lecturer's guidance.

Disclaimer

TVET Nexus Hub is an independent project and is not affiliated with KNEC or any college. If you believe any material on this site infringes your rights, please contact us and we will review it and remove it promptly.
